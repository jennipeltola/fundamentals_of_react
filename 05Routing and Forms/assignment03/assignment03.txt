The reason why I took the course was to learn more about React, because my
skills were seriously lacking after one course in TAMK. I couldn't program 
even simple apps without AI's help, so my goal was to fix that.

My bachelor's thesis will focus on the importance of accessibility and 
user experience for neuroatypical children by creating a web application of 
learning games, which was also my motivation for the course and the practice 
gained through the assignment. The app will include artwork from my autistic 
6 year old daughter, who is also the main target for the purpose of the thesis. I used this assignment to experiment with some of her art and added different 
colors and fonts on each of the pages to see which one she might have liked 
the most.

The biggest challenge for me was not to use AI tools as much as I've relied 
on them previously. I mainly used AI for a couple of debugging issues, which 
was a great improvement for me and with some online tutorials, I got to 
rehearse CSS language as well. I still made a few mistakes and made some 
styles global to simplify things, which taught me to try a better approach 
on the final project assignment.
